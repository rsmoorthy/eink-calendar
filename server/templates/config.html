Layout configuration

These values control exactly where everything is drawn on the 400×300 canvas. Coordinates are pixels from the top-left. This is ordinary YAML: delete a key, or comment it out with #, and it stays deleted. What is shown is the saved file as you wrote it, comments and key order included; Reset to defaults deletes {{ config_path }} and restores the shipped defaults listed below.

An image needs at least one of width or height; with neither, it is drawn at its own pixel size and may land off-canvas. Give both to force an exact size, or just one to keep the file's aspect ratio — a 200×50 logo at width: 64 is drawn 64×16. A side of 0 or -1 also means "derive this one".
