Tamil months

Tamil months begin at solar ingress (Sankranti), which drifts by a day or two from year to year, so these start dates are maintained by hand. One entry per line: YYYY-MM-DD,EnglishName,TamilName. Dates outside this table render a dash for the Tamil month and date.

Note the final entry: dates after the last start date are covered by the last month until the next Sankranti is added. Extend this table before the end of the last year listed to avoid dashes.
